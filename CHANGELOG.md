# Prime Capitol app — what changed in each version

Every time the website goes live, the version number goes up and a line is added here in plain words.

- **Patch** (1.0.**1**): fixes and wording only. Nobody has to learn anything new.
- **Minor** (1.**1**.0): a feature was added or works differently. Check the manuals.
- **Major** (**2**.0.0): a big redesign. Everyone gets new training.

You can see which version you're on in **More** (bottom of the page) and at the top of **Help**. When a newer one is live,
the app shows a "Version X is ready · Update" bar. The team can see who still needs to update on **People & access**.
Each manual says which version it was written for.



## 1.4.0 · Nov 24, 2026

- **Pick up and return props from ChatGPT or Claude.** The assistant now follows up on props requests: it says what is
  packed & ready and what is due back, and offers the next step. At the pick-up spot you can sign for the props in the chat
  (or say what's missing, and the arts department is told). To return, take one photo of everything and attach it in the
  chat, say if anything broke, and it's returned. The arts department still sees when the photo was taken.
- **Gear from ChatGPT or Claude too.** Ask "is there a camera free?" and it shows what's free for your shoot, with photos.
  On the shoot day it lists every piece for you to check, asks for one photo, and takes it out for you; returning works the
  same way (one photo of everything in the case, batteries charging).
- **It understands the studio better.** Houses are booked only for filming, never for meetings or planning; "I need a day"
  means filming time.
- **Planning that problem-solves.** "A slot for me, Ariel and Pedro" means 2 hours each, back to back. If the day you want
  is full, ChatGPT/Claude suggests a shorter house block plus offsite for the rest, separate times, or the next day with room.
- **"Did you film?"** After each shoot, one tap in the app (or just tell ChatGPT/Claude "we filmed") so the team knows who filmed.
- **Booking up to about 2 weeks ahead** (Schedule settings: how far ahead creators can book). The team and priority bookers can go further.
- **Problem solving when a time is taken (ChatGPT/Claude):** the closest free time, the other house, the waitlist, or asking to swap (never with priority bookings). A booking that isn't approved comes back with the team's reason and new times.
- **Smarter props in ChatGPT/Claude:** it can read the whole closet, match a photo, offer free alternatives when a prop is taken, and file problem reports with a photo. Cards have Request / Add / Take buttons.
- **Faster and more photos.** Prop searches and video-idea checks answer quicker, and photo cards show on searches and the closet too.

## 1.2.0 · Nov 24, 2026

- **Book any time, not slots.** Pick your own start and end at a house in quarter hours (e.g. 1:15 to 3:15). Each person on a booking gets up to 2 hours: for 4 hours add one person filming with you, for 6 hours two, all on **one booking** that lists everyone. Friends don't have to confirm; it shows in their app too. The calendar's bar under each day shows how free each house is (bright = free, faded = partly free); tapping a day shows each house as a timeline with the free stretches in gold.
- **Google Calendar changes show up within seconds.** Google now tells the app the moment something changes in the linked calendars (it used to check every 15 minutes). A safety check still runs every 30 minutes and renews the live link by itself.
- **Use Prime Capitol from ChatGPT or Claude.** More → **ChatGPT & Claude** has the link and the steps. Sign in with Google, tap Allow, then ask it to find props (with photos), check the schedule, book time, request props or check your shoots. While you brainstorm a video, it can check which props we already have for each scene. Talk to it normally, however you text: "film with Justin and Rolly tomorrow", "book c2 sat at 5", "add the red phone to my saturday shoot", "cancel the one with Kyle", "yes to Jordan's invite", "how do I return stuff?". It works out who and what you mean (nicknames, typos and short forms too), assumes 2 hours each, finds times that work for everyone, asks if something could mean two things, and suggests what's worth doing next (props deadline coming up, something to bring back, an invite waiting). It asks before changing anything, and the same rules and approvals apply. App reviewers get a practice demo account (owner turns it on in More → ChatGPT & Claude).
- **What creators ask for (owner).** When someone asks ChatGPT or Claude for something the app can't do yet (a feature, a prop we don't have), the AI offers to pass it on and, if they say yes, it's counted anonymously so you can see what's most wanted: Team → Help & records → **What creators ask for** (Features / Props wanted). No names are kept, only the month, and anyone can turn it off in More → ChatGPT & Claude.
- **Custom props with a picture.** In ChatGPT, creators can make a picture of the custom prop they want and send it with the request; the arts department sees it on the request. The AI never gives a date for custom props: the arts department follows up separately (e.g. on Slack).
- **No more check-in on the shoot day.** No selfie, no check-in or wrap step: just film. If something went wrong the schedule team can still mark a no-show after the shoot started.

## 1.1.0 — November 23, 2026
