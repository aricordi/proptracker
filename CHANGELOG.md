# Prime Capitol app — what changed in each version

Every time the website goes live, the version number goes up and a line is added here in plain words.

- **Patch** (1.0.**1**): fixes and wording only. Nobody has to learn anything new.
- **Minor** (1.**1**.0): a feature was added or works differently. Check the manuals.
- **Major** (**2**.0.0): a big redesign. Everyone gets new training.

You can see which version you're on in **More** (bottom of the page) and at the top of **Help**. When a newer one is live,
the app shows a "Version X is ready · Update" bar. The team can see who still needs to update on **People & access**.
Each manual says which version it was written for.



## 1.2.0 · Nov 24, 2026

- **Book any time, not slots.** Pick your own start and end at a house in quarter hours (e.g. 1:15 to 3:15). Each person on a booking gets up to 2 hours: for 4 hours add one person filming with you, for 6 hours two, all on **one booking** that lists everyone. Friends don't have to confirm; it shows in their app too. The calendar's bar under each day shows how free each house is (bright = free, faded = partly free); tapping a day shows each house as a timeline with the free stretches in gold.
- **Google Calendar changes show up within seconds.** Google now tells the app the moment something changes in the linked calendars (it used to check every 15 minutes). A safety check still runs every 30 minutes and renews the live link by itself.
- **Use Prime Capitol from ChatGPT or Claude.** More → **ChatGPT & Claude** has the link and the steps. Sign in with Google, tap Allow, then ask it to find props (with photos), check the schedule, book time, request props or check your shoots. While you brainstorm a video, it can check which props we already have for each scene. Talk to it normally, however you text: "film with Justin and Rolly tomorrow", "book c2 sat at 5", "add the red phone to my saturday shoot", "cancel the one with Kyle", "yes to Jordan's invite", "how do I return stuff?". It works out who and what you mean (nicknames, typos and short forms too), assumes 2 hours each, finds times that work for everyone, asks if something could mean two things, and suggests what's worth doing next (props deadline coming up, something to bring back, an invite waiting). It asks before changing anything, and the same rules and approvals apply. App reviewers get a practice demo account (owner turns it on in More → ChatGPT & Claude).
- **What creators ask for (owner).** When someone asks ChatGPT or Claude for something the app can't do yet (a feature, a prop we don't have), a few words are counted anonymously so you can see what's most wanted: Team → Help & records → **What creators ask for** (Features / Props wanted / Confusing). No names are kept, the AI tells the person when it passes something on, and anyone can turn it off in More → ChatGPT & Claude.
- **No more check-in on the shoot day.** No selfie, no check-in or wrap step: just film. If something went wrong the schedule team can still mark a no-show after the shoot started.

## 1.1.1 · Nov 23, 2026

- Offsite shoots booked in the app now go into the **Stromedy Talent (Cliff 1)** calendar as "Name - Offsite Filming", the way the team already writes them (they used to go into the mixed Stromedy calendar). Moving one there keeps it offsite.

## 1.1.0 — November 23, 2026
- **Team log** (Team / Admin menu): every approval, denial, move, swap, cancel, priority booking, blocked time and Google
  Calendar card the team sorted, with who did it and when. **Undo** puts it back if the time is still free.
- **People not on the app yet** (People & access → Not on the app yet → + Add someone): a name only, no access. Their Google
  Calendar shoots show under their own name (e.g. Moe), and the team can book for them. When they join, Change their email.
- Fixed: when a repeating event in the old Google calendars was moved to a new time, the app could lose its blocked time
  (the old and new times shared one block), so the slot looked free and got booked again. Lost blocks are put back.

## 1.0.0 — November 23, 2026
The first numbered version. Everything that was live on Nov 23, plus:
- Version number in the app (More, Help, problem reports), an Update bar when a newer version is live, and each person's
  version on People & access.
- Booking by slots: pick how many hours, then who films in each slot (friends not on the app can be added by name).
- Team meetings in the old Google calendars are ignored, plus a "Not filming" list for anything else.
- Offsite shoot sheet fixed on iPhone.
- Wording fixes: strikes are in More; tapping a name on Requests opens that booking; the equipment team's guide points to
  How it works; "Open record" only shows to people who can open it.
- Manuals for creators, the arts department, the equipment team and admins (PDF + live pages), a 3-page quick guide and a
  training presentation, checked line by line against the app.
- Polish pass on every screen: one page header and one empty-screen style everywhere, switches instead of tick boxes, the
  laptop sidebar on every page, pill rows that run to the edge, a tidier More page, the prop page calendar, the gear picker,
  approval cards, the flags list, Rooms (was Locations).
- Same status words everywhere (Requested → Being prepared → Packed & ready → Picked up → Returned; "Waiting for approval";
  "Put it back"). Problems a creator reports show at the top of the props request.
- Team: must pick who a booking is for; Move can start from any date ("Show more times"); the Requests day view shows
  heads-ups and links to the Schedule to book or block.
