Privacy policy

Prime Capitol app · Last updated October 30, 2026

The Prime Capitol app is an internal, invite-only tool that Prime Capitol's creators and production team use to book filming time, props and gear. This page explains what the app keeps, why, and who can see it.

What we keep

  • Your Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile photo, so you can sign in and so the team knows who booked what.
  • Your profile: a photo you upload and your channel name, if you add them.
  • Your bookings: filming slots, props and gear requests, check-ins, video names you type, and their history.
  • Photos you take in the app: pack, pickup, return and set photos, with the time they were taken, to show items went out and came back.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, a code for your phone so we can send you booking updates.

How we use it

Only to run Prime Capitol's productions: managing the shooting schedule and the props and gear inventory, reminding people about their shoots and returns, and keeping a record of who had what. We don't sell your information, show you ads, or share it with anyone outside Prime Capitol except the services below that run the app.

Services that run the app

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud store the app's data and photos and send notifications.
  • Google Gemini powers the optional ✦ AI helper. When you use it, the text you type (for example a video idea) and the list of company props are sent to it to suggest props or times. Your email address and other people's details are not sent.

The company Google account

Prime Capitol connects one company Google account (for example bookings@primecapitol.ca) to the app. The app uses it only to add, change and remove shooting events in Prime Capitol's shared calendar and to send booking emails from that account. It does not read anyone's inbox. Prime Capitol can disconnect it at any time in the app or in that Google account's security settings (Third-party connections).

Who can see what

Creators see their own bookings and the shared schedule. Prime Capitol's team (props, equipment, admins and the owner) see what they need to do their jobs. Access is controlled by your role.

How long we keep it

Proof photos are deleted automatically after 6 months. Booking records are kept while you work with Prime Capitol so the history stays complete. Ask us and we'll delete or correct your information when it's no longer needed.
