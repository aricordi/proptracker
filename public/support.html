Support

Prime Capitol app by ARB Creative

The Prime Capitol app is how Prime Capitol's creators and production team book filming time, find props and request them for a shoot. It is built and run by ARB Creative for Prime Capitol, and it's invite-only.

Contact us

Email support@arbcreative.co. We usually answer within one business day. Inside the app you can also tap More → Report a problem; it sends us a screenshot of the screen you're on.

Using Prime Capitol in ChatGPT or Claude

  • Connect: add the Prime Capitol app in ChatGPT (or a custom connector in Claude with https://proptracker.arbcreative.co/mcp), then sign in with the Google account Prime Capitol invited.
  • "Not invited" when signing in: you need a Prime Capitol account. Ask the Prime Capitol team to invite the email you sign in with, or choose a different Google account.
  • It asks before changing anything: bookings, prop requests, moves and cancellations always need your OK, and bookings are approved by the schedule team.
  • Photos, pickups and returns happen in the Prime Capitol app on your phone.
  • Disconnect: in the app (More → ChatGPT & Claude → Disconnect) or in your assistant's settings.

Common questions

  • I can't sign in: open the app in Safari or Chrome (not inside Instagram, Gmail or another app), turn off content blockers, and use "Use a different Google account" if the wrong one is picked.
  • Custom props: requests for props we don't have are sent to the arts department, who follow up with you about details and timing. There's no guaranteed date.
  • Your information: see the privacy policy.
