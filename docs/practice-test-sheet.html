Practice Mode Test Sheet

Say each line to ChatGPT (voice mode) or Claude. Tick it when the answer matches. Nothing you do here reaches the arts or equipment teams.

Before you start

  1. Open the app. If a bar says "Version 1.5.0 is ready", tap Update.
  2. Go to More → ChatGPT & Claude and turn on Practice mode. The card turns amber.
  3. Start a new chat in ChatGPT or Claude with Prime Capitol turned on (ChatGPT: tap + and pick Prime Capitol, or type @Prime Capitol).
  4. Begin your first message with "Prime Capitol," (for example "Prime Capitol, who am I?"). Without the name, ChatGPT often answers from its own memory and never asks the app. Once it has used Prime Capitol in a chat, it usually keeps using it.

Prime Capitol, who am I?
You see it use Prime Capitol, then your name and the words PRACTICE MODE. If it doesn't mention practice, stop and turn it on in the app first.

Book Cliff 2 at a time you saw someone else has.

Book c2 tomorrow at 5-ish.

When you're done

  • In the app, check My shoots and My requests: nothing new from your test should be there.
  • Ask Maanik and the arts team: they should have had no notifications.
  • Turn Practice mode off (More → ChatGPT & Claude).
  • Say "Who am I?" again: it should no longer mention practice.
