Prime Capitol

One app for every shoot

Creators book house time, get props and take gear by texting a chat. The arts and equipment teams run everything from one place, and you see who's filming, who isn't, and what the closet needs.

The pitch in three lines

  1. Creators just text it."Thursday 5 PM at Cliff 1 with Kyle, and props for a séance video." The app finds the time, invites Kyle and lines up the props.
  2. Nothing goes missing without a name on it.Every prop and kit is photographed out and back, ticked piece by piece, and every change is saved with who did it.
  3. You run the studio from numbers, not group chats.Who's filming more or less, who no-shows, what runs out, what to buy. Approvals in one tap.

Why people can trust it

  • Nothing a creator booked is ever deleted; removals can be put back.
  • Every change is logged with a name and time.

  • The AI can't change anything itself: every action is a person's tap.

The 2-minute demo

Show it on your phone in this order. Each step is one tap or one sentence.

  1. 0:00Open the chat, type a booking."Saturday 3 PM at Cliff 2 with Ariel." It finds the slot and invites Ariel.
  2. 0:20Ask for props for an idea."Props for a haunted séance." Tick two, add them to the shoot.
  3. 0:45Open the Calendar tab.Free spots per day; your shoot shows "Waiting for OK".
  4. 1:00Switch to Maanik's view (View as Equipment).The request is there with the creator's record. Approve.
  5. 1:20Show the shoot day.Selfie to check in, one photo to take a kit, Return everything at the end.
  6. 1:40Finish on the numbers.Admin dashboard: who's filming more or less. Prop insights: what to buy.

Training: who needs what

  • Creators: nothing. The chat shows three tips the first time and answers "how do I…".
  • Arts: the one-page Team guide plus the crew sheet for the clean-up.
  • Equipment + admins: the Team guide and a 5-minute hands-on tour.
  • Anyone stuck: tap ✦ AI and ask, or Report a problem.
