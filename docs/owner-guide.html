Booking shoots

Bookings are confirmed instantly when the time is free. Two people can never get the same slot, even if they tap at the same moment.

My shoots (Oct 20): everyone has a list of just their own shoots, soonest first (Today, This week, Later), with what each still needs: props and gear show a gold ✓ with where they are, or a + with the last day to add them. Check in is right there on the day. Find it on Book ("Your shoots" at the top, then All my shoots), on Home next to "Coming up", or Me → My shoots. Past shoots are on the second tab.

The houses and slots

  • Cliff 1 (2375 Cliff Road) and Cliff 2 (2371 Cliff Road). A booking takes the whole house.
  • Nine 2-hour slots a day, from 7–9 AM to 11 PM–1 AM.
  • Creators get one house slot a day (2 hours). The schedule team can book more for someone.
  • Offsite shoots can be any hours (in half hours) and don't count toward the daily limit.
  • Sundays: open to everyone, but no crew.

How a creator books

Book → Book a shoot → pick "Just me", "+ 1 friend" or "+ 2 friends" → pick a day (or "Soonest") → tap Cliff 1 or Cliff 2 next to a time.

With friends, each person gets their own 2-hour slot, back to back. Your friend's slot is held for 20 minutes so they can confirm with one tap. If they don't confirm, it opens to everyone.

Or tap ✦ AI and say "4 hours with Alex next week, evenings": it finds real free times.

Priority days

Thursdays and Saturdays at Cliff 1 are for the priority group. Unclaimed priority slots open to everyone on the Sunday before, at 6 PM. You set the group in Schedule settings. Only creators and you are listed. You follow the lock too when you book for yourself (tick yourself to have priority); you and the equipment team can still book a creator into a locked slot by hand.

Moving, swapping, cancelling

  • Changed your mind? Cancelling within 10 minutes of booking is always free.
  • 48 hours or more before: moving or cancelling is free.
  • Less than 48 hours: moving isn't possible and cancelling counts as a no-show (a strike). The only free way out is a swap with another creator who accepts.
  • When the team moves or cancels someone's shoot, it never counts against them.
Monthly slots are switched off for now. The plan is that each creator gets 4 guaranteed Cliff 1 slots a month through a fair "wishlist + draw". Until that's built, every booking is just a shoot.

Gear

Creators book gear by the hour in two ways (Oct 21): grab-and-go kits (bundles, booked whole, what most shoots use, including the drone kits) and single items. In All gear, the equipment team sets each piece to Internal only (the default: team use, or only inside a kit) or Creators can book (creators see it under "Single items" and can book the ones not in a kit, picking how many). The app won't let the team put a booked piece into a kit, lower its count, or make it internal while creators have it booked. Older "add-on extras" still work and show with the single items. At pickup the creator ticks every piece; anything missing is reported to the equipment team. A return photo is required. Gear is late 30 minutes after the booked end.

Shared gear for friends filming back to back (Oct 20, your rule: one booking, everyone responsible): when friends are booked one after another at the same house, the person adding gear sees Share this gear with your friends? with each friend ticked. One gear booking then covers the whole block (e.g. 1–5 PM for two 2-hour slots): one pickup, one return. Any of them can do the pickup check and the return. While it's out, whoever signed for it at pickup holds it (if it's late, only they are paused from booking more gear). When it comes back, the person returning it ticks Who used this gear?; only the people ticked are responsible if it was late or a piece is missing, and the others get a notification saying who was ticked so they can tell the equipment team if it's wrong. Only the person who booked it can change or cancel it (and add a friend later); a friend can tap Take me off this gear before pickup. If a friend cancels their shoot they come off it automatically. Friends get a phone notification "Gear shared with you". Being on shared gear doesn't make you responsible: using it does. If the last person in the block drops off, the gear hours shrink so their slot is free for someone else.

Handing gear over on the spot (Oct 20, your choice): if someone else needs the gear before it goes back to the cage (say a creator on an offsite shoot walks in), the people using it open the gear and tap Hand it over, pick the person and until when. The group's booking ends right there (no return photo). The new person gets a notification, opens it, ticks every piece and signs, the same as a normal pickup; anything missing at that moment goes on the group's record. It only works if nobody else has that gear booked before the time picked, and the new person can't be paused or have late gear. They bring it back to the cage normally, with the photo.

Anyone with late props or gear can't book more until it's back.

Late return fee (company rule, Oct 27): gear that comes back late carries a fee of 10% of its value. Gear is late 30 minutes after its booked end time (the same moment the app already called it late). The fee goes on the last person to have it: whoever returned it or handed it over, or, if the team returned it for them, whoever signed for it at pickup. The app does not charge anything: it keeps the record so you can enforce it. Props are logged the same way but with no fee for now.

  • Values: the equipment team fills in each piece's replacement value in All gear (an amber box lists pieces with no value yet). A kit's value is the total of its pieces. Each booking keeps the value from the moment it was booked, so later price changes don't rewrite history.
  • Creators see it: the kit's value and the 10% rule show on the kit page, when they send the request, and on their gear booking. Reminders before the end time and when it's late mention the fee too. All three tutorials teach it (creators get a pop quiz).
  • The log: Analytics → Late returns (you and admins): how many times, per creator, gear vs props, what's still out, and the value of the late gear. Every late return shows when it was due, when it came back, exactly how late, the value and who had it last. Tap one to see the photos and act: Excuse it (an exception, with a reason; it stops counting but stays on record), send a note, or give a strike. Download this log as a spreadsheet for your records. A creator's report card also shows their late returns.

Bin-by-bin check (cleaning up the inventory)

Take a bin out, open it in the app (Team → Bins & storage → tap the bin, or scan its QR sticker) and tap Check this bin. Tick Here for each prop you see. Not here takes a prop out of that bin and puts it on the Needs a bin list at the top of the Bins screen ("Was in B: 5"); nothing is deleted. Something in the bin the app doesn't list: It's in the app → move it here, or It's not in the app → add it. Done with this bin unlocks once every prop is ticked and marks the bin "✓ Checked" with the date and who did it. The Bins screen shows "N of M bins done".

On the day, and strikes

  • Check in: opens 15 minutes before the slot. The creator takes a set photo with the camera.
  • At check-in they can report the slot before (ran over into their time, or left a mess, with photos and a short message in their own words, shown to the team on the report). Reports go to the team, who dismiss them or turn them into a strike.
  • Wrap: "what did you film?" Checked in but no wrap still counts as filmed.
  • Missed check-in: the creator has 12 hours to explain. Then the team marks "They filmed" or "No-show".
  • Strikes: 2 strikes in 30 days pauses props and gear booking for 14 days. The equipment team or an admin can lift a pause early (Schedule → Team → Creators).
  • Admins (Oct 20): on Admin → Creators, tap a person to see every flag in the last 30 days, this month or 90 days: no-shows, missed check-ins, late cancels, starting more than 15 minutes late, running over, leaving a mess, late props, late gear, strikes. From there an admin (or you) can Give a strike (props, gear, schedule, behaviour or other, with a reason; it counts toward the automatic pause), Remove a strike (stays on the record as removed, with the reason; an automatic pause lifts if they drop below the limit), or Pause bookings for 3 to 30 days with a reason (stays until it ends or is lifted).

Google Calendar

The golden rule: the app only ever changes events it created itself. Events typed straight into Google Calendar, old or new, are never moved, changed or deleted by the app.

What goes where

  • App → calendar, instantly: when anyone books, moves or cancels a shoot, or the team blocks time in the app, it shows up in the shared Google Calendar right away.
  • Calendar → app, every 15 minutes: the app reads the calendar. If an event it imported earlier was moved or deleted in Google Calendar, the app's schedule follows by itself.
  • New events typed into Google Calendar need a person to say who it's for and which house. A blue banner on the Schedule screen ("N new events in Google Calendar to sort") opens the import screen.

Odd times and double bookings from the old calendar

  • An event like 2:30–5:00 PM keeps its real time. On the app's schedule it shows "Really 2:30–5 PM" and holds every slot it touches (1–3 and 3–5), so nobody can book over it.
  • Two old events at the same house at the same time are both imported and marked Clash on the schedule, so the team can sort it out with the creators. New bookings in the app can never clash.

Connecting it (one time)

  1. On a computer: Book → settings (sliders) → Google Calendar.
  2. Step 1: tap the link and press Enable (turns on the Google Calendar API; free).
  3. Step 2: copy the app's email shown there. In Google Calendar open the shared calendar's settings → Share with specific people → add it → "Make changes to events".
  4. Step 3: same settings page → Integrate calendar → copy the Calendar ID → paste it in the app.
  5. Step 4: Save & test. "Connected ✓" means it worked.
  6. Tap Import from the calendar and sort the existing events.

The Google Calendar screen (from Schedule settings) also lists everything the app has added, each with a Remove button, and anything Google refused, to try again.

AI helper (one chat)

Every creator screen (Home, Book, Browse, Me) has a purple ✦ AI button at the top. It opens one chat that does two jobs:

  • Help me find props for my video idea: describe the video, then keep going ("something for a séance"). It suggests real props and gear from our catalogue, never repeats, and you tick what you want and add it to a shoot.
  • Help me find an available time for my shoot: "4 hours with Alex next week, evenings". It shows real free times with a Book button. It never makes up a time; the schedule picks them.

Plan my shoot with me (the first choice in the chat) walks a creator through everything in order, with a progress bar at the top:

  1. Time: pick a shoot they already booked, or ask for a new time and book it right there.
  2. Props: describe the video; the AI suggests real props that are free that day. Tick what you want.
  3. Gear: a plain list of the gear that's free for the shoot's hours (the AI doesn't pick gear). Tap + to add. When there's no gear in the app yet, it says so and you skip.
  4. Review: a summary, then Review & send opens the normal "Your shoot" page already filled in (even the video name). The creator can add, remove or change anything there, and nothing goes to the teams until they tap the button on that page.

Plan it with AI also appears on each booked shoot (the shoot page, under "For this shoot") and on Home's "Props or gear for…" reminders. It starts straight at the props step for that shoot.

When you plan a shoot that already has requests, the chat first shows "Already on this shoot" (what you've requested and the gear you're holding) and never suggests those again. Gear you already hold shows "On this shoot ✓".

If that shoot's props request is still "Requested", the last step offers "Add the props to my sent request". It opens that request's Change screen with the new picks marked NEW. Nothing changes until you tap Save changes.

No switch to pick. Creators just type and the chat works out what they mean:

  • "Props for my Saturday shoot" → props for that booked shoot, with free or taken for its day.
  • "Find me 2 hours this week" or "4 hours with Kyle" → free times.
  • "A séance video, filming Friday with Kyle" → both: props first, then times.
  • Short follow-ups ("evenings only", "something with candles") carry on with whatever the chat was doing.

If it guesses wrong, a small "Meant a time to film?" or "Meant props for this?" link under the answer tries the other one. Everything still works together: tick props, then book a time in the same chat and add the ticked props to that shoot with one tap. The chat stays open in the background until you tap New chat.

Budget: $50 a month in total, and 25 uses per person per day. If either runs out, AI help pauses and search and browse keep working. Change both in Team → AI helpers. A typical use costs well under a cent.

The AI can't book, change or see private things. It only suggests; the app checks everything it says.

Notifications

Phone notifications are free. On iPhone they only work when the app is added to the Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen). Each person turns them on in Me → Notifications.

  • Instant: friend invites and confirmations, swap requests, props packed, requests declined, team changes to your shoot, reports and strikes.
  • Reminders: an hour before a shoot, when check-in opens, missed check-ins, the last day to add props or gear, props due back, gear due back or late.

Not getting them? (Oct 21) In Me → Notifications, tap Send me a test: the app now says exactly what happened ("Sent to this phone", "Your phone refused it", or "not signed up any more"). Then tap Test with the app closed (10 s) and swipe the app away right after: iPhones don't show a banner while the app is open on screen. If it still doesn't show, check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Prime Capitol (Allow + Banners on), turn off any Focus / Do Not Disturb, always open the app from its Home Screen icon, and as a last try turn notifications off and on again in the app. Every notification the app sends is now recorded, so "Read server logs" shows whether it left our side.

Test someone else's phone (Oct 23): you and admins can open any person (Team → People or a creator's record) and scroll to Notifications. It lists the phones they have signed up and when each last opened the app, and has a Send them a test notification button that says whether Google accepted it. If Google accepted it but nothing showed on their phone, the problem is on the phone: they should open the app from the Home Screen icon, go to Me → Notifications, turn it off and on again. If "last opened" is days old even though they use the app, they are probably opening it in Safari instead of the Home Screen icon.

Email notifications are on hold until you have admin access to the Google Workspace account (alex@primecapitol.ca).
