Who's who

Everyone signs in with Google, and only people you (or an admin) invite can get in. Your rule (Oct 20): anything that could break the app (maintenance, settings, connections, bulk tools) is yours alone; everyone else gets only what their job needs. You set each person's role on Team → People & access.

Admin home = Dashboard (Oct 20): admins open on a Dashboard (you see it on your Team page under Creator scoreboard). It's a creator scoreboard: every creator (and you) ranked by shoots filmed (only creators and you are counted, here, in Analytics and on the Creators list; admins, the arts department and the equipment team never appear, even if they booked something), compared with the typical creator (the middle number: half filmed more, half less). Each one is marked Posting more, Typical or Posting less, and separately Needs attention for behaviour (a no-show, late cancel, strike, damaged or missing props or gear, showing up under 70%, or paused), with the reason. Tap anyone for their report card (their rank, month by month against the typical creator, their flags, what they filmed). Then buttons to Analytics (tabs: Creators first, then Late returns, Flags, Teams, and Trends last), which works like YouTube Studio's analytics: pick one date range (last 7 or 28 days, 12 weeks, 6 or 12 months, this or last month); every number says whether it went up or down compared with the same stretch of time before (green = better, red = worse); tap a card to show it on the chart (the dashed line is the average); tap a column, a person, a house or a flag type to zoom in, and Back to zoom out. Tabs: Overview, Creators (each person, what they filmed), Flags (abandoned shoots, running over, mess, skipped check-ins, late or broken returns, not confirming) and Teams (the arts department and equipment team: what is waiting, packed on time, check-in speed). The table icon opens the Advanced view: break any number down by creator, house, week or month, flag type, flag or staff member, sort any column, and download it as a spreadsheet and the Settings sheet (every rule the app runs on, its value, and who can change it). Acting on flags (Oct 21): on a creator's report card, tap the Flags or Serious flags card (or the red reasons line) to jump to every flag they have, filtered To do / Handled / Excused / All (plus "Serious only"). The Flags tab has the same list for everyone (it opens on To do). Tap any flag for what happened (the shoot and times, check-in time and set photo, what they filmed, their explanation, the next creator's report and photos, return photos, what came back damaged, who used shared gear) and what was already done about it, with who and when. Then act: missed check-ins and house reports get They filmed / No-show · strike and Dismiss / Uphold · strike; any other flag gets Send them a note (a phone notification, with a ready-made message you can edit), Give a strike for this (one per flag; counts toward the automatic pause), Remove the strike that came with it, Excuse it (it stops counting in every number, but stays on record and can be counted again) or Mark as handled. Every action except "handled" needs a short reason. "Needs you now" also counts flags nobody has looked at in the last 28 days. Their tabs: Dashboard, Queue, Gear, Schedule, Props. The arts department isn't asked to do one. When the tour ends, everyone lands on the Home page.

Edge-case check (Nov 5): four reviewers went through the whole app looking for problems before you hit them; about 50 were fixed. The main ones: Props-only creators and unconfirmed imports never get check-in reminders or "missed check-in" flags; switching someone to the Full app never turns their past shoots into misses; excusing a flag removes the strike that came with it; a strike never shortens a pause an admin set; two people can't give two strikes for the same thing; the chat finds your shoot by its date ("cancel my shoot on the 19th"); sending props no longer stops you adding more; a failed selfie check-in can be retried; gear only goes out for approved shoots; friend holds run out by themselves; swaps follow the priority days and the guideline.

Chat test (Nov 6): before launch, a robot sent the real AI chat about 180 messy messages ("tmrw @ 7", "c2", "me and kyle", "yes", "asdfgh", "are you a bot", "samedi à 17h", jokes and trick messages) on the practice site, then on the chat screen itself. What changed:

  • It's honest. Asked "are you a bot?", it says it's the app's AI assistant, not a person. It never says something is booked, approved or "all set": only the card does that.
  • It doesn't make things up. It never claims a time is free or that we have a prop; the real free times and props show right under its reply. It doesn't invent door codes, Wi-Fi help, "first shoot of the month", or weekdays. For keys, codes and house problems it says to message Maanik.
  • It reads messy typing better. "me and Kyle saturday" finds back-to-back times; "the 5pm one" or "the second one" picks from the times shown; French works ("samedi à 17h", "demain 9h30"); "the 31st" in November means December 31; two things in one message ("book Friday 5pm and I need a cop costume") get both answers; a bare "ok" or "yes" with nothing asked gets "Yes to what?" instead of a guess.
  • While planning a shoot, "skip" or "that's it" moves on to the review, and "cancel" or "stop" asks first (the shoot stays booked).
  • A safety filter on the server removes anything the AI must never say, even if it tries: "I've sent your request", "Done!", "I'll keep an eye on it", "we've got a few couches", "Saturday is wide open". The real free times and props always show right under its reply.
  • Just a day or a time ("next sat", "the 31st", "5:30") goes straight to the time finder, which reads dates exactly. "Props for Saturday" with no idea gets "What's the video about?". A friend's name that isn't in the app is pointed out.
  • Result after four rounds (180 messy messages each): no dead ends, no errors, and the only flagged words left were harmless ("all set to check in").

Priority bookings (Nov 3)

People on Schedule settings → Books without approval (Kyle / Stromedy) get their bookings approved at once. Kyle's own Google Calendar is already read in (the Stromedy calendar). When a priority booking needs a slot someone else already has:

  • The team books it: on Requests tap Priority booking… (or tap the person's name in a day), then Book it for Kyle. Pick what happens to the other person: move them to another free time that day (their props and gear move with them) or cancel their shoot. No strike. They get a neutral "Schedule change" notification and a card in their chat (with "Find another time" or the option to shoot somewhere else), and after a cancel their next request goes first (marked "First in line" at the top of Requests).
  • Kyle's Google Calendar: if an event from his calendar lands on someone's slot, it shows at the top of Requests as Calendar events on a booked slot with Book it for Kyle. The team decides; nothing is moved by itself.
  • Kyle in the app: tapping a booked slot shows Priority booking: take this slot. The other person is handled the same way.

Gear: first come, first served (Nov 3)

Gear can't be booked ahead at all in this first version. It's tracked by the equipment team and checked out on the day. Shoots only get props and costumes added.

  • On the shoot day (from an hour before), they tap the Take gear card above the typing box in the chat, tap + on what they're taking, then Take it: tick every piece (Missing? if something isn't there) and take one photo. The equipment team gets a "Gear taken" notification.
  • Due back (Nov 3): at the houses, when the shoot ends (the next person may need it). Offsite, by end of day (midnight; change it in Schedule settings → Gear). It's late 30 minutes after that.
  • Houses-only kits: on a kit's edit page, switch on Houses only and it can't go offsite. Usual kit for Cliff 1 or Cliff 2 puts it first for shoots at that house. Both are optional.
  • Booking gear ahead is switched off. If you ever want it back: Schedule settings → Gear → Creators can reserve gear ahead.
  • Returns, late returns (and the late fee record) and check-in work as before.

  • What's in it: a copy of every Prime Capitol prop (not your personal ones), the gear kits, categories, bins and booking rules, plus pretend people (Riley, Kyle, Jana, Sam the admin, the arts and equipment test people…) with shoots and requests already in progress.
  • Be anyone: sign in with your own Google account, then tap Switch in the orange strip at the top and pick a person. You really become them: every screen, button and rule is the one they'd get. Switch back to "You (owner)" any time.

Booking shoots
