Cleaning up the props

The photo import put every prop in the app. Now we make sure each one has the right name, the right number and the right bin, and we get rid of junk and doubles. About 10 seconds a prop.

Where to find it

Open the app, tap Admin (bottom right). The gold card at the top:

Clean up props912 to check

Fix names, counts and bins. Remove junk and doubles.

Then tap the big gold Check props one by one.

Every prop, the same 4 checks

  1. Is the name right?Tap the name and type over it. Use the words someone would search for: "Red rotary phone", not "IMG_2034".
  2. How many do we have?Tap − or +. Count what's really on the shelf.
  3. Which bin is it in?Tap Change and type the bin number. Amber "No bin" means it still needs one.
  4. Then pick one of the three buttonsat the bottom of the screen:

Skip and ‹ Previous are at the top of the screen. The top pill lets you work one bin at a time, or only props with no bin, odd names or no photo.

It's a double: the one question

Tap the other entry (the closest names show first, or type to search). Then the app asks:

We have more than oneTwo real chairs → one entry, count 1 + 1 = 2
It's the same one, photographed twiceOne chair, two photos → one entry, count stays 1

Shortcuts

  1. Same photo twiceThe app finds entries with the very same picture, or the same name in the same bin. Tap the one to keep, then Make it one prop. "Not the same, leave them" hides that group.
  2. Quick listEverything in one list. Tap a name to rename, − / + to change the count. Tick several to Move to bin, set a Category, Combine or Remove them all at once.

You can't break anything

  • Remove never deletes. It goes to Removed props with a Put back button. Right after removing, Undo shows at the top for a few seconds.
  • Every change is saved with your name, so mistakes are easy to find and fix.
  • The app won't let you lower a count below what creators have booked, or remove a prop that's out on a shoot.

Tips for the first day

  • Stand at the shelf with your phone and go bin by bin (top pill → pick the bin). The list is already in bin order.
  • Two people? Each takes different bins so you don't check the same props.
  • Wardrobe: put the size in Description, size… (for example "Size XL").
  • The progress at the top of Clean up props counts everyone's work: ✓ = checked.
  • Stuck? Tap ✦ AI on the Admin page and ask "how do I combine two props?", or tap Report a problem.
