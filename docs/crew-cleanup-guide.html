Cleaning up the props

The photo import put every prop in the app, with some doubles, missing bins and odd names. The app finds the problems for you. Your job is mostly to answer yes or no, in this order.

Where to find it

Open the app, tap Admin (bottom right). The gold card at the top:

Clean up props912 to check

Fix names, counts and bins. Remove junk and doubles.

Inside, the jobs are listed under Do it in this order, each with how many are left. Do them top to bottom; each one makes the next shorter.

  1. Combine doubles

    The app has already compared every prop. It shows a group of photos that may be the same thing (two, three, sometimes more). Tap any photo to see it big. All are ticked: untick any that's different, then:

    None are the sameCombine 3

    Then one more question:

    One chair, photographed 3 times

    One prop, photographed 3 times

    Three real chairs

    We have 3 separate copies

    The AI looks at the next groups while you work and says what it thinks, but it never combines anything: you always tap Yes. The green and violet lists at the top (exact same picture, "the AI thinks these are the same") let you check many at once and confirm. Not sure? Skip.

  2. Put props in their bin

    Props with no bin, but whose photo folder is named like a bin. Check the photos, then tap Put them in A2 (or put them all).

  3. Quick approve

    Props that already look right: real name, a bin, a photo. 30 at a time, already ticked. Untick anything that looks wrong, then:

    Approve 30 ✓

  4. Work bin by bin

    Stand at the shelf, open that bin in the app: everything in it on one screen. Tap anything wrong (name, count, not here, junk) to fix it right there. When the rest is right:

    Everything here looks right ✓

    Then the app takes you to the next bin. A name in yellow (like "IMG_2034") needs a real name first. Tap ✦ Suggest a name and the AI looks at the photo for you.

When you fix one prop

Tap it. Fix the name (or ✦ Suggest a name), how many (− / +), where it is (type the bin number), then Save ✓. Junk (not a prop, blurry photo, tape or batteries, gone)? Remove. Already in the app under another entry? It's a double.

✦ AI never changes anything by itself. It only suggests; you tap to accept.

Spot a double while you work?

Open the prop and tap ⋯ (top right, next to Edit):

  • Find duplicates: the app shows props that may be the same thing. Tick them, tap Combine, pick which one to keep.
  • Merge with another prop: type its name.
  • Mark as checked, or Remove (you can put it back).

You can't break anything

  • Remove never deletes. It goes to Removed props with a Put back button, and Undo shows at the top for a few seconds.
  • Every change is saved with your name.
  • The app won't let you lower a count below what creators have booked, or remove a prop that's out on a shoot.

Tips

  • Two people? One does Combine doubles on the couch, the other walks the shelves with Work by bin.
  • Wardrobe: put the size in the description (for example "Size XL").
  • The progress at the top of Clean up props counts everyone's work.
  • Stuck? Tap ✦ AI on the Admin page and ask "how do I combine two props?", or tap Report a problem.
